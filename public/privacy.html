JobFinder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-15

JobFinder is a small, private job-listing tool built for a limited group of specific, individually-approved people (not the general public). This page explains what data it handles and why.

Who can use it

Access is restricted to a fixed allowlist of specific Google account email addresses, set by the app owner. Signing in with a Google account not on that list is rejected — there is no open sign-up.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email address, obtained via Google Sign-In, used only to check you're on the approved list and to identify you while signed in.
  • Nothing else about you. No name, no profile photo beyond what Google's sign-in flow itself may briefly show, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.

What we don't collect or store

Job listings you scrape (titles, salaries, employer names, etc.) are pulled live from third-party job sites and processed entirely in your browser. None of that data is saved on our servers — closing the browser tab or ending your session clears it. Downloaded Excel files are generated in your browser and saved directly to your own device; they never pass through our servers either.

Cookies

A single cookie is used: a signed session token that proves you're signed in. It's httpOnly (not readable by page scripts) and expires after 7 days. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Third-party services

Signing in uses Google Sign-In (Google's own privacy policy applies to that step). Some job sites are fetched through Firecrawl, a web-scraping API service, which sees the URLs being fetched but not your identity or login details.

Data deletion

Since no personal data is stored beyond your email address (used only for the access check) and an expiring session cookie, there's nothing further to delete on request — removing your email from the allowlist immediately revokes access.
